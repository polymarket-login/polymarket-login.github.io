Polymarket

Polymarket is a prediction-market platform where participants trade contracts tied to the outcomes of future events. Market prices can be viewed as an implied probability, and participants can compare different outcomes across topics such as politics, economics, sports, culture and technology. Product access and rules depend on the applicable Polymarket service and jurisdiction.

What Is Polymarket?

Polymarket offers prediction markets in which people buy or sell outcome contracts based on their views about future events. The contract price fluctuates as participants trade and react to information. Prices are not guarantees that an event will happen; they represent market pricing at a particular time.

How Event Contracts Work

A Market Question

A market is created around a defined event and resolution criteria, so participants know what outcome is being measured.

Yes or No Outcomes

Depending on the market, contracts represent outcomes such as Yes or No, or one of several possible results.

Trading and Pricing

Participants trade with other market participants. Prices move as demand, supply and information change.

Market Resolution

After the event is resolved according to its published terms, contracts settle based on the outcome.

Understanding Contract Prices

In a simple binary market, a contract priced at 70 cents may be interpreted as roughly a 70% market-implied probability. A contract that resolves in the affirmative may pay $1 per contract, while a contract that resolves negatively may pay $0, according to the market's terms. The price can change before resolution, and implied probability is not certainty.

Polymarket US and Other Services

Polymarket operates separate services with distinct legal, technical and geographic conditions. Polymarket US is a CFTC-regulated exchange, while Polymarket International is a separate blockchain-based service with its own access restrictions and terms. Do not assume that products, protections, funding methods or eligibility are the same across services.

Transparency and Market Information

Market pages may display outcome prices, volume, liquidity, event details and resolution criteria. Reviewing the full market terms is important because the wording, source used to resolve an event, timing and exceptions can affect settlement.

Things to Consider

Trading event contracts involves risk. Market prices can move quickly, liquidity may be limited, and a position may lose some or all of its value. Markets can also be affected by uncertainty about event definitions or resolution sources. This page is general informational content and is not financial, investment or legal advice.
